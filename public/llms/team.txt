# Team Paste (`team`), Tiny Little Office

A Pokémon Showdown export as a self-contained team card. A document is a link: https://office.thin-sky.cloud/team/#doc=<percent-encoded TLO>

A fence with language showdown holds the original Pokémon Showdown export. Separate sets with a blank line.
The editor displays cards and edits the export text; it does not validate battle-format legality.

Templates: A little rain team.

## Shapes

- `showdown` a Showdown team export, original Showdown text inside a showdown fence
