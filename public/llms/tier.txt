# Tier List Maker (`tier`), Tiny Little Office

Rank text and emoji cards in editable tiers, all inside a link. A document is a link: https://office.thin-sky.cloud/tier/#doc=<percent-encoded TLO>

The first table has Tier and Item columns. Rows are grouped by the first appearance of each tier; a row with an empty item keeps that tier visible. Cards are text or emoji. Edit controls move or reorder them; desktop dragging moves a card to a tier.

## Shapes

- `ranks` ordered tier rows, a Tier and Item table; empty items preserve empty tiers

## Example: ordered tier rows

```
Tier | Item
--- | ---
Unranked | 🧀 Cheese
Unranked | 🍓 Strawberries
```

Every shape as a document, a link and JSON: https://office.thin-sky.cloud/examples/tier.json. The whole description: https://office.thin-sky.cloud/llms-full.txt
