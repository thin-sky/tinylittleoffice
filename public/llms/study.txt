# Study Packs (`study`), Tiny Little Office

Flashcards, cloze questions, and a practice session inside a link. A document is a link: https://office.thin-sky.cloud/study/#doc=<percent-encoded TLO>

One table: Front | Back | Hint. A row is a card; ==word== inside a question is a cloze deletion.
The document contains all answers. Practice progress stays in memory for the visit and is not shared.

Templates: Toki Pona · first words, Make a study pack.

## Shapes

- `cards` question and answer cards, a first table with Front, Back and optional Hint columns

## Example: question and answer cards

```
Front | Back | Hint
--- | --- | ---
jan li moku. | A person is eating. | One possible reading; li marks the predicate
ona ==li== pona. | li | Supply the missing predicate marker
```
